VEGETABLE BIRYANI RECIPE
========================

A fragrant, layered basmati rice dish cooked with spiced vegetables and
saffron-infused milk. Serves 4.

INGREDIENTS

FOR THE RICE
  - 2 cups (400 g) aged basmati rice
  - 4 cups (1 litre) water
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 4 green cardamom pods
  - 1 tsp salt

FOR THE VEGETABLE MASALA
  - 3 tbsp ghee or neutral oil
  - 2 medium onions, thinly sliced
  - 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  - 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  - 1 cup cauliflower florets
  - 1 medium carrot, diced
  - 1 medium potato, diced
  - 1/2 cup green peas
  - 1 tsp cumin seeds
  - 1 tsp red chilli powder
  - 1 tsp garam masala
  - Salt to taste

METHOD

STEP 1 - SOAK THE RICE
  Wash the basmati rice and soak for 30 minutes, then drain.

STEP 2 - PAR-COOK THE RICE
  Boil water with whole spices and salt. Add rice and cook 70% done. Drain.

STEP 3 - MAKE THE VEGETABLE MASALA
  Fry onions, add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes and spices. Add vegetables
  and cook until just tender.

STEP 4 - LAYER THE BIRYANI
  Layer masala then rice in a heavy pot. Drizzle saffron milk and ghee.

STEP 5 - DUM COOKING
  Seal and cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes.

STEP 6 - SERVE
  Serve hot with raita and lemon.

PREP TIME : 30 minutes
COOK TIME : 50 minutes
TOTAL TIME : 80 minutes
SERVES     : 4
